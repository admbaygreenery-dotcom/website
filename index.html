MEET THE OWNER

Hi, I'm Nathan

Ten years in the industry and still happiest outside. I started Bay Greenery to bring the kind of careful, personal landscape work that's hard to find — the kind where someone actually walks the yard with you, hears what you need, and stays involved from the first sketch to the last sprinkler head.

I grew up loving the outdoors and that hasn't changed. When I'm not on a job site you'll usually find me on a Bay Area trail, in the garden growing whatever's in season, or testing a new recipe in the kitchen with what I just picked.

Every yard we touch gets the same approach: thoughtful design, honest work, and a finish I'd be proud to call my own.
