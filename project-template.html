Willow Glen Backyard Reimagine

San Jose, CA Landscape Design & Build 6 weeks Completed 2025

A growing family in Willow Glen wanted a backyard that worked for everyone — somewhere the kids could run, the dogs could relax, and the parents could host dinner under string lights on a Friday night. The existing yard was a tired patch of grass with broken irrigation and a sloped corner nobody used. We started with a full design conversation, walked the yard, and came back with a plan that turned the slope into a terrace, added a paver patio sized for a real dinner table, and replaced the old lawn with a healthy mix of low-maintenance plants and a small artificial turf zone for the kids.

What we built: a leveled paver patio with built-in seating wall, a tiered planting bed along the slope, a new drip irrigation system on a smart controller, low-voltage path and accent lighting, and a small artificial turf play zone. The family started using the yard every evening within a week of the install.

This is the kind of project we love most — a yard that gets used twice as much as it used to, and that looks like it has always been that way.
