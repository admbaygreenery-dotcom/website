Pavers

A paver patio or walkway is one of the highest-impact things you can add to a yard. It's the part of the landscape you'll actually walk on, sit on, and put furniture on every single weekend. So when we install pavers, we obsess over the base. The vast majority of paver problems — settling, lifting, weeds, drainage pooling in the wrong corner — come from a base that was a little too thin, a little too soft, or a little too uneven. We build a base you can drive a truck across, then lay the pavers on top. That's how you end up with a patio that still looks new ten years later.

  • Patios, walkways, pool surrounds and driveways
  • Concrete pavers, natural stone and permeable options
  • Engineered base with proper compaction and grading
  • Polymeric sand joints that resist weeds and ants
  • Edge restraints that keep the field tight for the long haul
  • Designed slope for clean drainage away from the house

When you walk on a paver install we've done, it should feel solid — no rocking pieces, no lifted corners, no soft spots. That feel is built into the base, not the top. We'd rather install fewer paver projects each year and do every one of them right than rush through and leave behind work that won't hold up.
