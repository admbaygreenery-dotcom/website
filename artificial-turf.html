Artificial Turf

A modern artificial turf install is nothing like the plastic mat people remember from a decade ago. The yarns look natural, the colors blend, and the feel underfoot is soft enough that even people who swore they'd never go fake change their minds the first time they walk on it. But the look is only half the job. The other half is what's underneath — and that's where most installs go wrong. We treat every turf install as a drainage and sub-base project first. Build that right, and the turf on top stays flat, drains cleanly, and lasts the full life of the warranty.

  • Pet- and kid-friendly turf options, including cool-touch yarns
  • Proper grading, sub-base and drainage so water doesn't pool
  • Tight seams and clean edges — no visible joins, no curl
  • Infill matched to your use (pets, play, low-traffic accent)
  • Optional pet de-odorizing infill and antimicrobial layers
  • Multi-year manufacturer warranties on the turf itself

If you've already had turf installed somewhere and it disappointed you, it's almost always because of what's underneath. We'd love the chance to show you the difference a properly built sub-base makes — and to install a lawn you can actually forget about.
