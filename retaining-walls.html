Retaining Walls

A retaining wall is more than a wall. It's the thing that turns an unusable slope into a flat patio, a terraced garden, or a level lawn the kids can actually play on. Done well, it disappears into the landscape and feels like it has always been there. Done poorly, it bulges, leans and leaks within a few seasons. The difference is almost always what you can't see: the footing, the drainage behind the wall, and whether it was engineered for the soil and water load it actually has to hold. We don't take shortcuts on any of that — because the wall is the part of your yard you want to think about the least.

  • Block, stone, poured concrete and timber walls
  • Proper footings and structural backing
  • Drainage built in — gravel backfill, drain pipe, weep outlets
  • Permitted, code-compliant builds where required
  • Terraced designs to turn slopes into usable space
  • Finishes that match your home and surrounding hardscape

A retaining wall is one of those projects where the cost difference between doing it right and cutting corners is small — but the long-term cost difference is huge. We'd rather spend the extra time on the base and drainage now than be the contractor you call when someone else's wall fails. Walls we build are still standing straight years later, and that's the only outcome we're interested in.
