Landscape Lighting

Most yards stop being used as soon as the sun goes down. Good landscape lighting changes that — and not in a way you have to think about. A well-lit yard feels warmer, safer, and somehow bigger at night. The trick is restraint: a few accent lights on the right trees, a soft wash along a path, an uplight that hits the front of the house at the right angle. We design lighting the same way we design landscapes — around how you'll actually live in the space, not how it looks the day after install.

  • Path and step lighting for safety and atmosphere
  • Accent uplighting on trees, walls and architectural features
  • Soft wash lighting for patios, decks and entertaining areas
  • Security-focused fixtures with motion sensors
  • Low-voltage LED systems — efficient and long-lasting
  • Smart controllers, timers and app-based control

When the lighting is right, you don't notice the fixtures — you notice the yard. That's always the goal. We aim for a system that turns itself on at dusk, hits all the right spots, and asks nothing of you in return.
