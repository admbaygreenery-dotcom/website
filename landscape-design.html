Landscape Design & Build

A good landscape isn't built — it's understood first, then built. When we start a design and build project, the first thing we do is listen. How will you actually use the space? Where does the morning sun hit? Where do the kids run, where do the dogs dig, where do you want to sit on a Sunday afternoon? Every plant, paver and irrigation line we put down should answer one of those questions. That's the difference between a yard that looks finished on day one and a yard that still feels right ten years in.

  • Site walk, measurements, and a conversation about how you use your yard
  • Custom 2D layout and plant palette tailored to your light and soil
  • Drought-tolerant and California-native options when they fit
  • Hardscape, irrigation and lighting planned together as one system
  • Full installation by our crew — one team start to finish
  • Walk-through and care guide so you know exactly what you're working with

When we hand the keys back, you should feel like the yard already belonged to you. That's what we're going for every time, on every project — and that's why most of our work comes from neighbors of people we've already built for.
