Irrigation Systems

In the Bay Area, water is the difference between a garden that thrives and one that limps along. A good irrigation system isn't just pipes and timers — it's the right amount of water, in the right spot, at the right time of day, adjusting itself as the seasons change. We design and install irrigation the same way we design landscapes: planned around your plants, your soil, your sun exposure, and how the system will hold up over time. Done right, you save water, save money, and stop thinking about it.

  • Drip irrigation for beds, planters and gardens — efficient and quiet
  • Lawn and turf sprinkler systems with proper head coverage
  • Smart controllers (Rachio, Hunter Hydrawise and similar) with weather-based scheduling
  • Zone planning that respects sun, slope, and plant water needs
  • Repairs, leak finding, valve replacement and seasonal tune-ups
  • Backflow inspection and rebate-program-ready installations

An irrigation system you can ignore is the goal. When yours works the way it should, you'll forget it's even there — except every month when the water bill comes in lower than you expected. That's what we're aiming for on every install.
