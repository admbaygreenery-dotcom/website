Fences

A fence does three jobs at once: it keeps things in, it keeps things out, and it sets the tone for the whole property. People notice a fence before they notice the yard behind it. That's why we treat every fence install like a piece of the landscape — not a stand-alone job. Material, height, post setting, hardware and finish all matter, and they all need to fit your home and your soil. A good fence stands straight for decades. A bad one leans, splinters and starts to look tired in two seasons.

  • Cedar, redwood and pressure-treated wood fencing
  • Composite and vinyl options for low maintenance
  • Metal — wrought iron, steel and aluminum
  • Custom heights, top caps, lattice and decorative finishes
  • Concrete-set posts engineered for Bay Area soil and wind
  • Gates with quality hardware that doesn't sag or stick

A fence is one of those things you should only have to think about when you decide to build it. We aim for installs that are tight, plumb and clean on day one — and that still look that way years later. We're happy to talk through the trade-offs of each material before you decide; the right answer depends on your goals, your budget and how much maintenance you actually want to do.
