Sod Installation

A great lawn isn't about the grass on top — it's about what's underneath. Most sod that fails fails for the same reasons: bad grading, compacted soil, an irrigation system that wasn't designed for the new lawn, and a watering plan that never made it past the first week. We treat every sod installation as a soil project first and a grass project second. That's the only way to hand over a lawn that's still thick, level and green a few seasons later — not just on installation day.

  • Removal of existing lawn or weeds, hauled away cleanly
  • Soil amendment and grading for proper drainage and water flow
  • Premium sod selected for your sun exposure and use (kids, pets, foot traffic)
  • Tight seams and edge work — no gaps, no stretched rolls
  • Irrigation check and adjust before we leave
  • Watering and mowing schedule so the lawn takes root the first time

A lawn is one of the simpler things we install — but it's also one of the most visible, and one of the easiest to do badly. We'd rather take an extra day on the prep than come back to repair a lawn that never quite settled. Most of our installs are still going strong years later, and that's how we'd like to keep it.
